## Phase 2c: LFO inlet (Matt, 2026-11-04, in chat; built offline in T035 / T036, not yet looked at live)

Decided by Matt on 2026-11-04 (this chat):
- D4: a second texture inlet takes an LFO, a time function: inlet 0 says where, inlet 1 says when. When connected it replaces the time function wherever there was one: `sin(phase)` for the point exciter, a constant 1 for the texture push (`Force * 2 (tex - 0.5) * T`). It is read per pixel, so a uniform LFO texture and a spatial one both work (Matt: "both"; docs: `vs_lfo` has texture out[0] and data out[1] per `f_droste.md`, `vs_wfg_3` outputs a float32 0..1 waveform texture; what `vs_lfo`'s texture contains is not documented, inferred uniform). Its own connected flag (`src_lfo`, via `mod_inlets` `vs_instate` + `state_param`, the `vs_displacement` / `f_weave` precedent); unconnected = today's behaviour.
- D5: a uni/bi toggle on the LFO read, modelled on `f_weave`'s `mmode` (uni = raw 0..1 and can only add; bi = `2 x - 1`, mid-grey neutral). Default not yet chosen (the library's toggles default to uni; Claude leaned bi for an oscillating drive).
- D6: the strike incorporates the LFO: "the lfo is what defines the character of the pulse the strike makes" (Matt). Claude's reading A, to confirm: at the strike frame the impulse is multiplied by the LFO value, per pixel (`strength * OUTLINE_AMP * mask * T`, or the point Gaussian times `T` with no texture), so the LFO's level, sign and spatial pattern at that instant shape the pulse. Reading B (the strike opens a timed window of LFO drive) would need a duration dial and a state channel (A holds the exciter phase, which freezes while connected, so it might be free then; inferred).
- Consequence to check: with the bi toggle, an LFO at mid-grey at the strike frame makes the strike zero, and a trough inverts it.
- Assumed, not asked: Force scales the whole pulsing push; no separate dial for the steady part.
- D7 (Matt, 2026-11-04, "yes remove it"): the point sine exciter is REMOVED, not just silenced when connected: no `Freq`, no `Amp`, no `frequency` / `amplitude` Params, no `EX_GAIN`, no phase accumulator (the state texture's A stays 1, a free channel). Consequence accepted: with nothing connected the module rings once from the load strike and decays (spec User Story 1, "keeps going", no longer holds). Claude's reading of "yes remove it": no replacement `rate` (auto-strike) dial, since none was asked for. The `f_cymascope` audio front end (T041) would now produce an LFO signal for the new inlet instead of `frequency` / `amplitude`.
- D8 (Matt, "yes"): `strength` scales the point strike too (unconnected), so the strike is `strength * STRIKE_AMP * gaussian` (point) or `strength * OUTLINE_AMP * mask` (outlines), times T.
- D9 (Claude's choices, flagged, not asked): the strike uses reading A (impulse times T at the strike frame); the LFO is a pure time modulator, so with inlet 0 unconnected it does not push at all (a spatial LFO that should act as a force field goes on inlet 0); the uni/bi toggle `lmode` defaults to UNI (0), the library convention (`f_weave` `mmode`, `f_vf_split`); Claude had leaned bi, for T037.

- D10 (Matt, "we should be able to remove the x and y dials now"): see T038.
- D11 (Matt, 2026-11-04): the strike becomes a HELD press with an envelope: "if we can respond to a press, then the button hold takes care of our AD and S: the A and D are from the incoming texture's luma, the S is the length of the button press, the R is from the speed + damp". Mapping: attack and decay = the luma of the time texture T on inlet 1 over the held frames (the upstream envelope source is retriggered by the same press, wired in the patch), sustain = how long the gate is held, release = the solver ringing out at its own Speed and Damp (no envelope in the module, no ADSR dials).
- D12 (Matt: "yes I think this is worth trying out", "go"): the held press drives DISPLACEMENT, not force. While the gate is held, u is pinned toward `strength * amp * T` by the outline mask (or the centre Gaussian) and, in the variant chosen (Claude, from the numbers below), v is killed at the pinned texels. A one-frame press on a field at rest is the old impulse. No gain constant: the amplitude is in field units (the outline's luma contrast times Strength times T). Measured offline in the mirror (a throwaway script, white square on inlet 0, 256^2, Damp 0.2, Edge 0.5): a held FORCE drive along the outline at strength 1 reaches max |u| 0.010 after 1 s (luma std 0.001; strength 10: 0.103), against 0.510 for the Force-1 texture push and 0.948 for the one-shot outline strike, because a unit force acts on only ~1000 texels, so a force drive would need a factor of ~100; the displacement pin avoids that. Pin variants over a 1 s hold with an attack / decay / sustain luma envelope: P1 (pin u only) energy at release 1.43e6, rebound after release max |u| 1.32; P2 (pin u, kill v) energy at release 1.7e5, max |u| 0.96 at 1.5 s; the one-frame press: 0.83 at 0.25 s, 0.13 at 2.5 s. Luma std of the held fields 0.04-0.10 against 0.005-0.017 for the one-frame press. Both variants stay finite and decay after release. How it LOOKS is Matt's (T043). Consequences: a pin replaces u at the pinned texels (a press on a ringing field does not add to the ring there); held outlines reflect waves like a rigid edge.
